Plant Leg Balance

NXTPlay Smart Footy prototype for testing the sensor mechanic. Not the production app.

Setup

How it works: tap Start, strap the phone to the lateral thigh of your plant leg (top of the phone towards the hip), and stand tall on both feet. Audio cues guide every rep, so you don't need the screen. Keep the volume up and the screen on.

Statue: on "Lift", stand on the plant leg with the kicking leg loaded in a backswing and hold.
Plant & Hold: on "Go", take one step into a firm plant and freeze in your follow-through.
Shadow Kick: on "Go", do a 3-step approach and a full kick with no ball, then hold the finish.
Getting ready

-tilt from ref (°)

Set results

Tap a rep to plot it. Score = 0.40 tilt + 0.25 wobble + 0.25 settle + 0.10 collapse (Statue: 0.65 tilt + 0.35 wobble). Pass = score of 70 or more plus the level's tilt and settle targets.
